# RICS Professional Standards

## Overview

The Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) sets global standards for the surveying profession. All Hillway work must comply with these standards.

**Hillway RICS Registration:** 900798
**Status:** RICS Regulated Firm

## Core Standards

### 1. Rules of Conduct

RICS members must:
- Act with integrity
- Always provide a high standard of service
- Act in a way that promotes trust in the profession
- Treat others with respect
- Take responsibility

### 2. Professional Statement: Conflicts of Interest

**Requirement:** Identify and manage conflicts of interest

**In Practice:**
- Disclose any potential conflicts to clients
- Obtain informed consent before acting where conflict exists
- Decline instruction if conflict cannot be managed
- Maintain information barriers where appropriate

**Common Conflicts:**
- Acting for landlord and tenant in same transaction
- Valuing property where fee depends on outcome
- Having financial interest in property being advised upon

### 3. Professional Statement: Complaints Handling

**Requirement:** Maintain complaints handling procedure (CHP)

**Hillway CHP:**
1. Complaint to be made in writing
2. Acknowledged within 7 days
3. Full response within 28 days
4. If unresolved, referred to RICS Dispute Resolution Service

**Include in engagement letters:** Reference to CHP and right to refer to RICS.

## Valuation Standards (Red Book)

### When Red Book Applies

Formal valuations for:
- Secured lending
- Financial reporting
- Investment/acquisition decisions
- Dispute resolution
- Taxation purposes

### Red Book Requirements

1. **Terms of Engagement** must include:
   - Identification of client
   - Purpose of valuation
   - Subject of valuation
   - Basis of value
   - Valuation date
   - Assumptions and special assumptions
   - Extent of investigation
   - Fee basis

2. **Valuation Report** must include:
   - Confirmation of competence
   - Basis of value definition
   - Valuation approach
   - Material considerations
   - Market commentary
   - Assumptions
   - Limitations on liability/publication

3. **Independence Requirements**
   - Valuer must be independent
   - Rotation after certain period for same client
   - Disclosure of previous involvement

### Bases of Value

| Basis | Definition | Use |
|-------|------------|-----|
| **Market Value** | Estimated exchange amount at valuation date | Most common |
| **Market Rent** | Estimated rental amount | Rent reviews, lettings |
| **Fair Value** | Per IFRS 13 | Financial reporting |
| **Investment Value** | Value to particular investor | Acquisition advice |

## Service Charge Standards

### RICS Professional Statement: Service Charges in Commercial Property

**Key Requirements:**

1. **Transparency**
   - Clear budgets provided annually
   - Year-end reconciliation within 4 months
   - Tenant can inspect invoices/contracts

2. **Fairness**
   - Costs must be reasonable
   - Apportionment must be fair
   - Only recoverable items charged

3. **Governance**
   - Regular review of contracts
   - Competitive tendering for major works
   - Reserve/sinking funds where appropriate

4. **Communication**
   - Annual budget meeting offer
   - Reasonable notice of major works
   - Prompt response to queries

### Common Service Charge Issues

- Management fees exceeding market rate
- Costs not recoverable under lease
- Unfair apportionment methodology
- Lack of competitive tendering
- Poor year-end reconciliation

## Lease Advisory

### Professional Statement: Real Estate Agency

**Key Points:**
- Duty of care to client
- Disclosure of material information
- Marketing at realistic price/rent
- Regular reporting to client

### Rent Review Conduct

- Act professionally and courteously
- Provide evidence to support case
- Consider alternative dispute resolution
- Keep client informed of progress
- Document all negotiations

## Property Management

### Professional Statement: Real Estate Management

**Key Requirements:**

1. **Management Agreement**
   - Written agreement essential
   - Clear scope of services
   - Fee structure transparent
   - Termination provisions

2. **Client Money**
   - Separate client account
   - Regular reconciliation
   - Interest policy clear
   - Prompt payment to clients

3. **Reporting**
   - Regular management reports
   - Financial statements
   - Arrears updates
   - Maintenance issues

4. **Insurance**
   - Appropriate PI cover
   - Client money protection
   - Buildings insurance (if managing)

## Documentation Requirements

### Engagement Letters

All instructions should have written terms covering:
- Scope of services
- Fees and payment terms
- Liability limitations
- Complaints procedure reference
- Termination provisions
- Conflicts disclosure

### File Notes

Maintain records of:
- Client instructions
- Advice given
- Key decisions
- Negotiations
- Site inspections

### Retention

Keep records for minimum 6 years (12 for sealed documents).

## Professional Indemnity

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Adequate cover for work undertaken
- Run-off cover if ceasing practice
- Notify insurers of potential claims promptly

## Continuing Professional Development

RICS members must:
- Complete minimum 20 hours CPD annually
- Record CPD activity
- Include formal and informal learning
- Maintain competence in practice areas

## Ethical Guidance

### Bribery Act Compliance
- No facilitation payments
- Gifts policy in place
- Due diligence on third parties

### Money Laundering
- CDD on clients
- Report suspicious activity
- Maintain records

### Conflicts of Interest
- Identify potential conflicts early
- Manage with informed consent
- Decline if cannot manage appropriately
